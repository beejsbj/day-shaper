Day Shaper

Design system

A toy for softly molding a what-if day. The sky is the protagonist: every surface reads against a live gradient keyed to real sunrise and sunset. Soft volume, thin light type, frosted glass, pastel clay. Source of truth: tokens.css; the app inlines these values for single-file offline integrity.

Tier 1 · Tokens

Sky phases

Four 3-stop gradients (zenith → mid → horizon), crossfaded by the solar clock over --dur-sky. Ink flips to --ink-dark-sky when mid-stop luminance < 0.45. Clouds fade with the light; stars rise as it dims.

Block pastels

Each block type is a two-stop clay pastel: -0 body, -1 shaded edge. Saturated enough to read on both skies, soft enough to never shout.

Type scale

One family (--font), hierarchy carried by weight and tracking, never bold. Hero is weight 200 — the thinnest thing on screen is the most important.

Tier 2 · Primitives

Matte pill · stone · scrim

Surfaces are soft matte — near-opaque, no blur. Glassmorphism is a forbidden mutation system-wide: depth comes from the sphere highlight and soft shadow, never from translucency.

Anatomy — stone. Canonical: 58px sphere, radial highlight at 35% 25%, emoji + 8.5px caps name. Parts: body / emo / nm. Tokens: block pastel pair, --shadow-float, --radius-orb. States: rest · grabbed (cursor + scale) · lifting (opacity .3, scale .88) · ghost (66px, follows thumb). Variant axis: block type only. Forbidden: borders, hard shadows, any non-spherical radius, text inputs of any kind.
Tier 3 · Unique specimens

The orb · sun · moon

Singular artifacts — never parameterized, never reused as containers. The orb is the dial's heart (porcelain by day, indigo by night); sun and moon ride the ring at their true hours.

Tier 5 · Compositions

A shaped day, both skies

The ring flattened to a band: sleep, free, work, eat, free, move, rest. In the app this wraps the oval dial; free time is the track itself showing through — absence of clay, not a component.

System laws. No forms, fields, or inputs — every interaction is touch on clay. No glassmorphism — surfaces are soft matte, never blurred translucency. Nothing casts a hard shadow. Weight, not boldness, carries hierarchy. The sky is never static. Free time is never drawn — it is revealed. Motion: springs for touch (--ease-springy, --dur-touch), slow linear fades for the sky (--dur-sky).
